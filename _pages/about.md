I am a recent graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, where I completed my undergraduate thesis under the supervision of <a href="https://ruhulsbu.github.io/" style="text-decoration:none;">Professor Ruhul Amin</a>, focusing on Natural Language Processing. My research interests center on data-centric AI, particularly in developing methods that make intelligent systems more interpretable and socially responsible. I also had the privilege of organizing the <a href="https://github.com/blp-workshop/blp_task1" style="text-decoration:none;">Violence Inciting Text Detection   (VITD)</a> shared task, endorsed by the first <a href="https://blp-workshop.github.io//2023/" style="text-decoration:none;">Bangla Language Processing (BLP)</a> workshop at EMNLP 2023, which engaged 88 participants from both undergraduate and graduate levels. As an AI enthusiast, I am eager to continue exploring how data-centric approaches can advance intelligent systems that better understand and assist people.
