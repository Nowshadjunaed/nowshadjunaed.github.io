I am a recent graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology. My interests broadly lie in building reliable, interpretable, and human-centered intelligent systems, with a strong emphasis on how data, models, and evaluation shape real-world outcomes. I have been actively involved in research and community initiatives, including organizing the Violence Inciting Text Detection (VITD) shared task, endorsed by the first Bangla Language Processing (BLP) Workshop at EMNLP 2023, which engaged 88 undergraduate and graduate participants. Drawing on both research experience and my background as a software engineer, I am motivated to explore how computational methods can better support people, decision-making, and complex systems.
